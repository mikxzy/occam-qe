Track B (LDA/PZ, norm-conserving) — required, confirmed by QE 7.5 itself (DEL C, C2/C3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
C2 attempted `elop=.true.` on the PBEsol/B2 structure (Track A pseudopotentials) and QE
7.5 refused outright: "Error in routine phq_setup (1): third order derivatives not
implemented with GGA" (PBEsol is a GGA). This is a hard QE limitation, not a numerical
issue -- see results/ELop_IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md. Track B is therefore required for any
EO calculation, not merely a smoke-test fallback.
